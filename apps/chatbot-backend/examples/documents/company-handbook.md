# Northwind Labs employee handbook

## Working hours

The office in Lisbon is open from 08:30 to 18:30, Monday to Friday. Core hours, when everyone is expected to be reachable, are 10:00 to 16:00 Lisbon time. Remote work is allowed up to three days a week; Wednesday is the team office day.

## Leave

- Every full-time employee gets 25 days of paid annual leave, plus Portuguese public holidays.
- Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year and expire on 31 March.
- Sick leave needs a doctor's note after the third consecutive day.
- Parental leave is 20 weeks at full pay for every parent.

Leave requests go through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance; your team lead approves them.

## Expenses

Travel and equipment expenses are reimbursed within 30 days of a submitted claim. Claims need the original receipt and must be filed within 60 days of the purchase. Hotel stays are covered up to EUR 150 per night; meals up to EUR 45 per day while travelling. Every employee has a yearly learning budget of EUR 1,200 for courses, books and conferences.

## Equipment

New joiners choose a 14-inch or 16-inch laptop on their first day. Laptops are replaced every three years. A home-office allowance of EUR 500 is paid once, in the first month.
